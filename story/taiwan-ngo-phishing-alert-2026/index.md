# 針對台灣 NGO 攻擊，請提高警覺

*[English version below](#english-version)*

OCF 近期確認多起針對台灣 NGO、媒體、政府機關的釣魚攻擊。根據目前掌握的資訊，這波攻擊與國家資助的駭客組織有關。請大家提高警覺，並立即檢查 Google 帳號的安全設定。

## 發生了什麼事？

攻擊者掌握台灣民主與人權倡議團體關心的議題，冒充熟悉的合作對象寄送釣魚郵件，誘導收件者點擊連結。

點擊後，可能出現 Google 登入或應用程式授權畫面。如果不慎輸入帳號密碼，或授權不明應用程式存取帳號資料，攻擊者就可能取得 Gmail、Google 雲端硬碟等資料的存取權限。

## OCF 做了什麼？

OCF 與 CSCS 社群接獲相關資訊後，已第一時間向 Google 通報。經 Google 確認，相關已知惡意應用程式已遭下架。為了避免更多人受到影響，我們仍建議大家主動檢查帳號設定。

## 現在花 1 分鐘，檢查你的 Google 帳號

### 方法一：直接檢查

1. 進入 [Google 帳戶的連結頁面](https://myaccount.google.com/linkedapps)。
2. 查看「連結的應用程式」。如果發現不認識、沒有使用過的應用程式，點進去並移除存取權限。

### 方法二：透過 Google 帳戶設定檢查

1. 點擊右上角的 Google 個人資料圖片。
2. 選擇「管理你的 Google 帳戶」。
3. 查看「連結的應用程式」。如果發現不認識、沒有使用過的應用程式，點進去並移除存取權限。

近期已有多起針對台灣公民社會的釣魚攻擊。攻擊者可能冒充你熟悉的合作夥伴，甚至使用你正在關心的議題取得信任。

收到可疑郵件時，不要急著點擊連結或授權應用程式。如果不確定郵件是否真實，請透過其他聯繫管道向寄件者確認。

如對這次事件有任何疑問，歡迎來信 [ssd@ocf.tw](mailto:ssd@ocf.tw)。

<h2 id="english-version">English version</h2>

OCF has recently confirmed several phishing campaigns targeting NGOs, media outlets, and government agencies in Taiwan.

Based on what we know so far, these attacks are linked to a state-sponsored hacking group. Please stay alert and check your Google account settings as soon as possible.

## What happened?

The attackers use topics relevant to these civil society organizations to send convincing phishing emails, sometimes impersonating trusted contacts or partners.

These emails contain links that may take you to a Google sign-in page or ask you to give an app access to your account. If you enter your password or grant access to a malicious app, attackers may gain access to your Gmail, Google Drive, and other account data.

## How did we respond?

OCF and the CSCS community reported these attacks to Google as soon as we received the information. Google has since confirmed and removed the known malicious apps involved. However, we still encourage everyone to check their account settings to stay safe.

## What should you do?

Take one minute to check your Google account.

### Option 1: Check directly

1. Go to [Google Account linked services](https://myaccount.google.com/linkedapps).
2. Review your linked apps. Remove access for any apps you don't recognize or have never used.

### Option 2: Check through Google Account settings

1. Click your Google profile picture in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Manage your Google Account**.
3. Review the linked apps. Remove access for any apps you don't recognize or have never used.

Stay alert. We have recently seen targeted phishing emails impersonating trusted contacts and using topics relevant to civil society organizations in Taiwan. Be cautious with suspicious links, and don't grant unfamiliar apps access to your Google account.

If you're unsure whether an email is genuine, verify it with the sender through a different communication channel.

For questions or concerns, please contact [ssd@ocf.tw](mailto:ssd@ocf.tw).
