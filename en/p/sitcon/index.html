About SITCON

The Students’ Information Technology Conference (SITCON) is organized by students, for students. It brings them together to share their experiences and show how they use technology in practice. Experienced participants can pass on what they know, students starting to make their mark can find inspiration in each other’s work, and newcomers can build a deeper understanding of computing. Everyone has a chance to put what they learn into practice and learn by teaching others.
