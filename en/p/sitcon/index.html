About SITCON

The Students’ Information Technology Conference (SITCON) is organized by students, for students. It gives students a place to share what they have learned and talk about how they put technology into practice. Experienced participants can pass on what they know, those who are finding their footing can draw inspiration from their peers, and newcomers can deepen their understanding of computing. The goal is for everyone to put what they learn into practice and learn from one another as both teachers and students.
